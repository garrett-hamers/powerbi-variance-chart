# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Power BI report authors and report consumers who need to compare actual results with prior-year, plan, or forecast values. Authors configure a reusable visual inside analytical reports; consumers scan variances, inspect details, navigate hierarchies, and cross-filter related visuals with mouse, touch, or keyboard.

## Product Purpose

Atlyn Variance Chart provides a free, reviewable, open-source visual for accurate variance analysis in Power BI. It makes favorable, unfavorable, and neutral movement understandable across ten chart styles while preserving Power BI conventions for formatting, selection, filtering, tooltips, themes, and accessibility.

## Brand Personality

Precise, trustworthy, and familiar. The visual should feel native to Power BI, keep attention on the data, and communicate analytical rigor without decorative distraction.

## Anti-references

Avoid proprietary or opaque analytical behavior, chart decoration that competes with the data, color-only meaning, nonstandard Power BI interactions, unexplained variance math, and dense layouts that hide labels or controls.

## Design Principles

- Make the comparison and its mathematical basis immediately legible.
- Preserve Power BI interaction conventions so the visual disappears into the report workflow.
- Represent every state honestly, including zero, null, negative, and extreme values.
- Keep analytical behavior consistent across every chart style and viewport.
- Prefer reviewable, deterministic behavior over hidden automation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA-compatible interaction and presentation within the Power BI host. Support keyboard operation, meaningful ARIA labels, visible focus, screen-reader context, high-contrast and report themes, non-color cues, reduced motion, and resilient layouts at small sizes.
